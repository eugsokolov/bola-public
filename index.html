The New Way to Moor

Your mooring, ready when you are

From the moment you book, your spot is yours. No searching. No guessing. Just navigate directly to your assigned buoy, activate it with a tap, and connect easily from deck level, turning one of boating’s most frustrating moments into one of its simplest.

How it works

From booking to mooring, effortlessly.

Mooring hasn’t changed much in decades. It can still mean uncertainty, searching for an available spot, and struggling to make the connection. Bola Buddy was built around a simple idea: there’s a better way.

Make it yours

Choose your destination, select your preferred mooring, and reserve it before you arrive. From that moment on, your spot is yours.

Sail straight to it

Follow the app to your assigned buoy and arrive knowing exactly where you’re going. No searching for an open mooring. No uncertainty.

Activate on arrival

When you’re ready to moor, activate your buoy directly from the app. Bola Buddy unlocks and prepares your reserved connection for you.

Connect with ease

The mooring point rises within easy reach at deck level, making it simple to grab, secure, and settle in – without the reaching, straining, or guesswork of traditional mooring.

All in one app

Stay on course. Book your spot, navigate directly, and order services without going out of your way.

Book in advance

Ahead of a trip, browse the map for available marinas, choose a mooring in your favorite spot and book it. 

Navigate to your buoy

With Bola Buddy’s navigation you will be able to see both your in-map Bola’s location and Bola’s LEDs lighting up in the horizon. 

The Quarterdeck

Request services directly from the app to your boat. Enjoy all that the marina has to offer from the confort of your cabin.

Frequently asked questions

What is BolaBuddy?

BolaBuddy is a smart mooring system designed for modern boating. It allows boaters to reserve, pay for, and securely lock their vessels to a mooring buoy using a mobile or web app. Once you reserve a mooring spot, the buoy is exclusively assigned to you, and nobody else can unlock it, ensuring that your mooring ball is available when you arrive. The system simplifies the mooring process and offers premium add-on services for an enhanced boating experience.

How does the smart locking mechanism work?

The smart locking mechanism is integrated into the mooring buoy and can be controlled through the BolaBuddy app. Once the boater arrives at their pre-reserved mooring spot, the buoy communicates with the boater’s device, and the locking mechanism is activated, securely fastening the boat to the mooring point. The locking system prevents unauthorized users from accessing the buoy.

Can I reserve a mooring spot in advance?

Yes! The BolaBuddy app allows you to reserve mooring spots in advance, ensuring a stress-free arrival. You can view real-time availability and secure your spot before setting sail. When you arrive at the marina, your assigned mooring buoy will be ready for you.

How do I know which mooring ball is mine?

As you approach your reserved mooring ball at the time of your reservation and come within 200 yards of it, the app will guide you to your spot. You can use the map in the app to navigate, with your mooring ball distinctively marked and positioned using GPS coordinates. Additionally, visual indicators like LED lights and optional sound signals will help you locate your assigned mooring ball, even in low visibility or crowded marinas, ensuring a smooth docking experience.

What makes BolaBuddy different from traditional mooring systems?

BolaBuddy eliminates the complexities of traditional mooring systems. Its deck-level design allows you to secure the buoy with one hand, without the need for reaching or bending. The smart locking mechanism and mobile app integration streamline the reservation, payment, and docking process, making mooring safer, easier, and more convenient.

How does BolaBuddy benefit marinas?

BolaBuddy provides marinas with a streamlined reservation management system that allows them to manage bookings, payments, and mooring ball inventory efficiently. It also offers revenue-enhancing opportunities through add-on services like provisioning and ice delivery, and dynamic pricing based on mooring location. The platform helps marinas optimize operations and increase revenue.

Can BolaBuddy accommodate different booking needs?

Yes! BolaBuddy supports flexible booking options, allowing boaters to reserve mooring spots on an hourly, overnight, or extended basis. This flexibility caters to different types of boaters while maximizing marina occupancy and efficiency.

How does BolaBuddy ensure secure mooring?

BolaBuddy’s proprietary locking mechanism ensures that only authorized users can access and secure the mooring buoy. The system uses the boater’s reservation details to authorize the locking mechanism, preventing unauthorized users from mooring at your spot.

Can I leave my mooring ball and return later?

Yes, if your mooring stay is for a day or longer, you can leave the mooring ball and lock your Bola Buddy to reserve it for your return. This ensures that your spot remains secure. You can lock and unlock it as many times as you need, allowing you to explore without worrying about losing your mooring or leaving your dinghy, paddleboard, or kayak as a placeholder.

How do the light and sound features benefit boaters?

The light and sound features help boaters find their reserved mooring balls even in challenging conditions like fog, nightfall, or crowded waters. These signals make mooring safer and more efficient.

Is BolaBuddy expanding to other locations?

Yes, BolaBuddy is expanding to popular boating destinations. Users can view available locations in the app and book mooring balls in advance.
